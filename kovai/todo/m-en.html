Wedding Extravagance
or Social Progress?

If Mahakavi Subramania Bharati were alive today, perhaps he would ask us not only how lavishly we celebrate, but how many lives our wealth could help transform.

“Why spend crores on a celebration that lasts a day, when the same money could help build a better future for generations?”

A question worth asking

A wedding is a beautiful celebration of two people and their families. It deserves joy, warmth and celebration. But should a family go into debt to impress others for one day? Could a portion of that spending create lasting value for the community?

🎵 Song: Wedding or Social Progress?

If Bharati Were Alive Today…

Chorus

Listen, my people! Listen, my friend! Pause for a moment and think! Crores are spent on a wedding day— What lasting good does that money bring? Is wealth meant only for one grand show? Must countless dreams be left behind? Think of the good that money could do; Plant seeds of change in the heart of humankind!

Verse 1 — Beyond wedding extravagance

Grand are the halls, bright are the lights, A thousand guests and endless displays; Gold and jewels are piled up high, While families borrow to win praise. If love is the heart of a marriage, Why must luxury define the day? Let two hearts join in simple joy— Let kindness guide the way we celebrate!

Verse 2 — Let us build businesses

Take a part of the wedding expense, Help a young dream become a start; Give a chance to a determined mind, Let enterprise give work to many hearts. Let new inventions see the light, Let honest businesses rise and grow; Farming, science and technology— Let our nation lead the world tomorrow!

Verse 3 — The gift of healthcare

Let us stand beside a child in need, Let no family face illness alone; Like St. Jude, let caring hospitals grow, Where hope and healing find a home. Let research move the frontiers forward, Let new discoveries light the way; And let a child without the means Receive the care they need today!

Verse 4 — If Bharati spoke today

If Bharati walked among us now, What fearless questions might he raise? “You work so hard to gather wealth— Why spend it all on outward praise? Poverty still waits outside our doors; Good education is not yet for all. You have resources in your hands— Now use your wisdom, answer the call! Live not only for your own applause; Help others rise, help humanity!”

Verse 5 — A promise to the next generation

More than gold, give children knowledge; More than status, give them skill. Give every girl the strength to stand, Give every young mind room to build. We do not say, “Do not celebrate”; We do not say, “Let joy depart.” Celebrate with care, spend with thought— Leave a better world in every heart!

Final chorus — A call for change

Celebrate love! Celebrate life! But do not drown in debt and show. A happy family is a wonderful start— Let the whole community grow! Build a school, support a clinic, Help a new enterprise begin; Fund research, protect young lives— Let compassion be our greatest win! Fear not change! Fear not change! Let courage shape the road ahead! Let waste grow less, let knowledge grow— Let hope rise high in every heart!

How can we turn this idea into change?

A meaningful social transformation can begin with one family's thoughtful decision.

Set a sensible wedding budgetPlan within your means. Do not take on debt simply to impress guests or compete with others.
Set aside a portion for goodChoose a meaningful amount for education, healthcare or a credible community initiative.
Create opportunities for young peopleSupport skills training, mentoring, apprenticeships and responsible small-business initiatives.
Give transparentlyCheck an organisation’s credibility, financial reporting and the real-world impact of its work before donating.
Start the conversation at homeAlong with “How much will the celebration cost?”, ask “What good could we do with a part of that money?”
Lead by exampleChoose a joyful, thoughtful celebration and show the next generation that generosity is a lasting family value.

A small family pledge

At our next family celebration, let us make these promises:

  • Avoid unnecessary extravagance.
  • Celebrate without creating a burden of debt.
  • Set aside what we can for education, healthcare or livelihoods.
  • Research the organisations we support and follow the impact of our contribution.

Living only for ourselves is not enough.

There is greater meaning in living for humanity.
Celebrate the wedding — but do not forget to uplift society.
