# Audiation

## What is Audiation?

**Audiation** is the ability to hear music internally without external sound.

It includes:

- Melody
- Harmony
- Rhythm
- Counterpoint
- Dynamics
- Instrumentation

Think of it as the musical equivalent of your inner voice.

---
