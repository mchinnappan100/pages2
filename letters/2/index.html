How to Use

Appreciation Letter Builder — quick guide
1
Fill in the form (left pane) Enter the recipient, title, body, closing, signature and date. The canvas updates live as you type.
2
Pick a style Choose Classic Gold, Modern Clean, Floral Accent, or Formal Blue from the Card Style dropdown.
3
Try a sample Click Sample to instantly fill the form with one of three pre-written letters. Click again to cycle through them.
4
Resize panes Drag the animated gold splitter bar left or right to give more space to the form or the canvas.
5
Pan the canvas Click and drag on the dotted background (outside the letter card) to pan around the infinite canvas.
6
Save & Export Save JSON stores all fields for later. Load JSON restores them. Export PNG and Export SVG download a high-quality image of the letter.
7
Toggle theme Use the 🌙 / ☀️ button in the header to switch between light and dark modes.
💡 Tip: Press Esc to close this dialog at any time.
