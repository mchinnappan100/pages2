A dedication to a visionary mind · 1927—2011

John McCarthy.

The mind that gave Artificial Intelligence its name.

Computer scientist, mathematician and visionary. McCarthy helped turn the question “Can machines think?” into a field of research—and gave generations of programmers a language for expressing ideas.

01 / The journey

Ideas that changed
the shape of computing.

From mathematical logic to interactive computing, McCarthy pursued a bold possibility: computers could do more than calculate—they could represent knowledge and reason about it.

1927

A curious mind begins

Born in Boston, Massachusetts, on September 4.

1948

Mathematical foundations

Earned a mathematics degree from the California Institute of Technology.

1955–56

AI takes a name

Helped propose the Dartmouth Summer Research Project, which helped establish AI as a field.

1958

LISP is born

Created LISP, a language that became central to symbolic AI research.

1960s

Interactive computing

Advanced time-sharing, helping make interactive access to computers practical.

1962

Stanford years

Joined Stanford, where he helped build a major center for AI research.

1971

Turing Award

Recognized by the ACM for major contributions to AI.

2011

A lasting legacy

Passed away on October 24; his ideas continue to influence computer science.

02 / Beyond LISP

A body of work far
beyond one language.

McCarthy helped shape the intellectual foundations and practical infrastructure of artificial intelligence.

01 · FOUNDATIONS

Artificial Intelligence

Helped establish AI as a research discipline and championed the goal of building machines capable of intelligent behavior.

02 · LOGIC

Knowledge & reasoning

Explored formal logic, situation calculus and ways to represent facts, actions and common-sense knowledge in machines.

03 · COMPUTING

Time-sharing

Advocated computer systems that let many people interact with a shared computer, rather than waiting for isolated batch jobs.

04 · MEMORY

Garbage collection

His early LISP work helped introduce automatic memory reclamation—an idea now familiar in many programming environments.

05 · AI SYSTEMS

The Advice Taker

Proposed a system that could accept knowledge expressed in formal logic and use it to derive conclusions.

06 · INSTITUTIONS

Building AI communities

Co-founded the MIT AI Project with Marvin Minsky and later founded Stanford’s AI Laboratory (SAIL).

03 / The language

LISP: code as data.
Ideas as expressions.

Created in 1958, LISP (LISt Processing) made symbolic expressions, recursion and the manipulation of programs themselves central to a programming language. Its influence reaches into functional programming, language design and AI research.

;; A tiny LISP example
(defun square (x)
  (* x x))

(square 5)
;; → 25
