A principle worth keeping

Productivity with care.

Higher yields matter, but lasting success also means healthy soil, reliable water, safe food, fair returns, biodiversity, and farms that can withstand changing conditions. The best solution is the one that works for the farmer, the place, and the next generation.

Local roots, global learning

Different places, shared priorities

There is no single farming formula. Climate, land size, water, crops, culture, infrastructure, and markets all shape the right choices.

For farmers in India

Explore locally suitable crop choices, monsoon and water planning, soil-health practices, efficient irrigation, farmer collectives and FPOs, storage and market linkages, and guidance from trusted KVKs and agricultural universities. Match every decision to your district, soil, water availability, and local advice.

For farmers around the world

Adapt to local climate and regulations; strengthen soil and watershed care, sustainable livestock and fisheries, safe mechanisation, post-harvest systems, digital access, farmer cooperatives, and routes to reliable markets. Share knowledge across regions while respecting local expertise.

Small steps, real progress

Your 90-day learning roadmap

Choose one practical improvement that fits your farm. Learn, test, observe, and share what works.

Days 1–30 · Understand your farm

Record your crops or livestock, soil and water situation, input costs, yields, weather risks, and current challenges. Ask a local agricultural expert or experienced farmer to review your plan.

Days 31–60 · Try one improvement

Test one manageable change—such as a soil test, mulch, a water-saving method, crop diversification, better storage, or a reliable digital record—on a small area first.

Days 61–90 · Measure and share

Compare costs, labour, water use, crop health, quality, and returns. Keep what works, adjust what does not, and share useful lessons with your family or farmer group.
