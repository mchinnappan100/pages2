01 / The journey

From a software start-up
to the launch pad.

Musk began in internet software, then put his proceeds into hardware that most investors thought was too risky: rockets and electric cars.

1971

Born in Pretoria

Elon Musk is born in South Africa on June 28.

1995

Zip2

Starts Zip2 with his brother Kimball, making online directory and map software for newspapers.

2002

PayPal and SpaceX

eBay buys PayPal, which grew from his X.com. The same year he founds SpaceX.

2008

Orbit, and Tesla

Falcon 1 reaches orbit on its fourth try, and Musk becomes Tesla's CEO.

2012

Dragon at the ISS

Dragon becomes the first commercial spacecraft to reach the International Space Station.

2015

A booster lands

A Falcon 9 first stage lands upright after launching a payload to orbit.

2020

Astronauts launch

Crew Dragon carries NASA astronauts to the ISS, the first crewed orbital launch by a commercial company.

2024

Catching a booster

Starship's Super Heavy booster returns to its launch tower and is caught by mechanical arms.

02 / The four ideas

Habits behind
the hardware.

Four engineering ideas show up again and again in how his companies work.

1

First principles

He often explains his approach as reasoning from physics and raw costs, such as pricing a rocket by its materials.

2

Reuse

Rockets that land and fly again turn a one-way, throwaway launch into something closer to air travel.

3

Vertical integration

SpaceX builds much of its hardware in-house, from engines to structures, so design and production stay close together.

4

Rapid iteration

Starship's test program accepts visible failures, including explosions, as the price of learning quickly.

03 / The ventures

Rockets, cars, satellites
and a few more bets.

Few founders have started or led companies in so many fields. These are the best known.

01 · LAUNCH

SpaceX

Founded in 2002 to lower the cost of spaceflight. Its Falcon 9 first stages now land and fly again.

02 · VEHICLES

Tesla

Musk joined in 2004 as an investor and chair, and became CEO in 2008. Tesla helped push electric cars into the mainstream.

03 · SATELLITES

Starlink

SpaceX's satellite internet network, with its first batch of satellites launched in 2019.

04 · PAYMENTS

X.com and PayPal

He founded X.com in 1999. It merged with Confinity, became PayPal, and was sold to eBay in 2002.

05 · NEUROTECH

Neuralink

Founded in 2016 to build brain-computer interfaces. The first human implant took place in January 2024.

06 · AI

xAI

Founded in 2023. Best known for Grok, its AI chatbot.

04 / The reusable rocket

Land it.
Fly it again.

On December 21, 2015, a Falcon 9 first stage landed upright after delivering a payload to orbit. Reuse had been a long-term dream of spaceflight. SpaceX made it routine.
