💡 About This Series
📖

Story-First Learning

Every unit opens with a read-aloud story. Children learn best when knowledge is embedded in narrative — this series honors that.

🤝

Parent-Child Together

Each unit has a dedicated Parent Guide explaining the developmental "why" and exactly how to run the lesson.

🎮

Play-Based Assessment

Every assessment is a game, not a test. Observation-based, joyful, and celebrating what children know — not punishing what they don't.

🧠

All-Round Development

Literacy, mathematics, science, social studies, emotional intelligence, and creative expression — no domain is left behind.

🌙

Dark Mode Ready

Every book has a dark/light mode toggle — comfortable for evening reading sessions.

🇺🇸

US Curriculum Aligned

Aligned with Common Core standards, NGSS science, and US kindergarten readiness benchmarks for American families.

🗓️ How to Use This Series

Recommended pace: one unit per week = one book every six weeks = complete series in about one year.

  1. Read the Parent Guide before each unit — it explains the developmental context and how to make the lesson effective.
  2. Read the story aloud with your child together — use an expressive voice, pause and ask questions, make it an event.
  3. Do the activities — gather materials beforehand so the lesson flows without interruption.
  4. Run the assessment as a game at the end of the unit — note what your child knows, not what they don't.
  5. Add to the portfolio — collect one artifact per unit. By Book 10 you'll have a stunning record of an extraordinary year.
