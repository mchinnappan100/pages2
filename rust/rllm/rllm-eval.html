rllm eval

Evaluate language models with perplexity, BLEU, ROUGE, accuracy, and side-by-side comparison.

Phase 9 ✓PerplexityBLEU · ROUGE-LCompare
← Back to Portal
Commands
CommandFlagsDescription
rllm eval perplexity <FILE>--model --strideMeasure perplexity of a text file under the model
rllm eval bleu--reference --hypothesisCompute BLEU score between reference and hypothesis
rllm eval rouge--reference --hypothesis --typeCompute ROUGE-1/2/L score
rllm eval accuracy--labels --predictionsClassification accuracy from label/prediction files
rllm eval compare--model-a --model-b --inputSide-by-side comparison of two models
Phase 9: Pure-Rust BLEU and ROUGE-L (n-gram counting + LCS). Perplexity via sliding-window log-softmax over GPT-2 tokens. Compare two model checkpoints side-by-side by perplexity. 8 unit tests verified.

Examples

rllm eval perplexity --model gpt2 test.txt
rllm eval bleu       --reference ref.txt --hypothesis hyp.txt
rllm eval rouge      --reference ref.txt --hypothesis hyp.txt --type rouge-l
rllm eval compare    --model-a gpt2 --model-b gpt2-finetuned --input prompts.txt
