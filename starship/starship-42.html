02 / The engineering idea

Don't just land it.
Catch it. Reuse it.

Traditional recovery approaches bring a vehicle down onto landing legs or a prepared surface. A tower catch attempts something different: use the launch infrastructure itself as part of the recovery system.

That means extraordinary precision — in vehicle control, timing, structural design, sensing and coordination between the ship and the tower.

The breakthrough is not only getting to space. It is building a system that can come back and fly again.
