Meet the problem solvers

Inventors You Should Know

🚗

Mary Anderson

Windshield wiper · 1903

She noticed how difficult it was for a driver to see through rain and snow and designed a manually operated windshield-cleaning device.

💭 Problem: Poor visibility
🍽️

Josephine Cochrane

Practical dishwasher · 1886

Frustrated by dishes being damaged during hand washing, she designed a machine that used water pressure to clean them.

💭 Problem: Washing dishes safely
🚦

Garrett Morgan

Three-position traffic signal · 1923

He developed an important improvement to traffic control by adding a warning position between stop and go.

💭 Problem: Safer traffic flow
💡

Lewis Latimer

Improved carbon filament · 1880s

His work helped make electric lighting more practical by improving carbon-filament technology.

💭 Problem: Practical electric lighting
🚆

Granville Woods

Railway electrical innovations · 1880s–1890s

His inventions and improvements helped advance electrical communication and railway systems.

💭 Problem: Better railway communication
🧲

George de Mestral

Velcro · 1940s

After seeing burrs cling to fabric and fur, he examined them closely and developed the hook-and-loop fastening system.

💭 Problem: Easy fastening
🛡️

Stephanie Kwolek

Kevlar · 1965

She discovered an exceptionally strong, lightweight polymer fiber that became known as Kevlar.

💭 Problem: Strong, lightweight material
📡

Percy Spencer

Microwave cooking technology · 1945

He noticed that radar equipment could heat food, an observation that helped lead to the microwave oven.

💭 Problem: Faster cooking
🖊️

László Bíró

Modern ballpoint pen · 1938

Inspired by quick-drying newspaper ink, he developed a pen using a tiny rotating ball to transfer ink to paper.

💭 Problem: Cleaner, faster writing
☕

Melitta Bentz

Paper coffee filter · 1908

She experimented with blotting paper to remove coffee grounds and bitterness from brewed coffee.

💭 Problem: Better-tasting coffee
📝

Art Fry & Spencer Silver

Post-it Notes · 1970s

A weak adhesive developed by Silver found a surprising use when Fry needed a bookmark that would stick without damaging pages.

💭 Problem: Temporary notes and bookmarks
❄️

Willis Carrier

Modern air conditioning · 1902

He developed a system for controlling temperature and humidity, laying important foundations for modern air conditioning.

💭 Problem: Temperature and humidity

Invention stories

Tiny observations. Huge consequences.

🚗

Mary Anderson saw a visibility problem

Imagine driving in snow or rain and having to stop repeatedly because you cannot see. Anderson's idea put a cleaning blade on the windshield and allowed the driver to operate it from inside the vehicle.

Think like an inventor:

What everyday problem have you experienced that could be solved with a clever mechanism?

🌿

George de Mestral looked closely

Burrs stuck to clothing and animal fur. Instead of simply pulling them off and forgetting about them, de Mestral examined their structure. The tiny hooks inspired a completely new fastening idea.

Think like a scientist:

Nature often provides engineering ideas. What could you learn by observing plants, animals, or insects?

The big lesson

You don't need to be a genius to start inventing.

You need curiosity. Great inventors observe the world, ask questions, try ideas, learn from failure, and keep improving.

👀

1. Observe

Find something that could work better.

❓

2. Ask

Turn the problem into a question.

🧪

3. Experiment

Build, test, fail, learn, repeat.

✨

4. Improve

Make the solution more useful.
